Car Rental Website Plan

Website Plan

This document provides the plan for the Island Drive Car Rentals website, including its purpose, audience, colors, typography, and home page layout.

1. Site Name

Island Drive Car Rentals

🚗 Island Drive

I chose the name Island Drive Car Rentals because the business provides affordable and convenient rental vehicles for people traveling around the island. The name is simple, memorable, and connects the business with island transportation.

Optional domain: islanddrive-rentals.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Island Drive Car Rentals website is to provide customers with information about available rental vehicles, rental prices, vehicle features, and booking information. Customers will be able to view different vehicles and learn how to contact the business to make a rental request.

The website will provide an easy and convenient way for visitors to find a vehicle that fits their transportation needs while visiting or traveling around the island.

3. Scenarios

Potential visitors may ask questions such as:

  • What types of cars are available for rent and how much does each vehicle cost?
  • How can I contact Island Drive Car Rentals to reserve a car?
  • Which rental vehicle would be suitable for a family trip?
  • What information do I need to provide when renting a car?

4. Color Schema

The website will use a simple color scheme that creates a professional and friendly appearance.

Dark Blue - #003049

Used for the header, footer, main headings, and important navigation areas.

Blue - #0077B6

Used for buttons, links, accents, and interactive elements.

Light Blue - #E8F4F8

Used for page backgrounds and sections to provide contrast and a clean appearance.

White - #FFFFFF

Used for content areas and text placed over dark backgrounds.

5. Typography

The website will use simple and readable fonts so that customers can easily find information about the rental vehicles.

Heading Font: Georgia

Georgia will be used for major headings and section titles. It gives the website a professional and welcoming appearance.

Body Font: Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, forms, and other general content because it is easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.
