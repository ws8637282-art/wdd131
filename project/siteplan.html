Samoan Culture - Website Planning Document

Site Name

Samoan Culture

I chose the name Samoan Culture because the website will focus on the traditions, food, language, clothing, values, and beautiful places of Samoa. The name is simple and clearly represents the purpose of the website.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to introduce visitors to Samoan culture and traditions. The website will provide information about Samoan food, traditional clothing, language, family values, customs, and important places in Samoa. It will help visitors learn more about Samoa and appreciate its culture and way of life.

Scenarios

  • What are some important traditions and customs in Samoan culture?
  • What traditional foods are popular in Samoa?
  • What traditional clothing do Samoan people wear?
  • What are some beautiful and important places to visit in Samoa?

Color Schema

#003049

Dark Blue will be used for headings, the header, navigation, and footer areas.

#F1FAEE

Light Cream will be used for the main page background and content sections.

#E9C46A

Golden Yellow will be used for buttons, highlights, borders, and other important accents.

Typography

Poppins will be used for headings, titles, and navigation because it is clean and easy to read.

Arial will be used for body text and paragraphs to make the information easy to read.
